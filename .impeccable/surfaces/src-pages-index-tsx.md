## Surface Intent

- Mode: Persuade.
- Audiences: people in India who receive digital vouchers and want to donate or claim them; recruiters and hiring teams evaluating a credible full-stack product demonstration.
- Primary action: Browse vouchers, continuing to `/browse` for public discovery.

## Direction

- Exchange Board: show one voucher moving through the donated, available, and claimed states so the exchange is understood before the visitor enters the operating product.

## Proof Constraints

- Keep proof factual and grounded in the working product: public browsing, community donation, claiming, and the supported wallet voucher exchange.
- Do not invent testimonials, customer logos, usage metrics, press, pricing, production-scale claims, or binding no-ads/no-tracking/no-monetization promises.
